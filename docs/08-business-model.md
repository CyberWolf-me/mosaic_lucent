# 8. Business Model

A SaaS model with four tiers.

## Free

- Limited AI generations
- Limited workspaces
- Limited voice minutes
- Basic diagrams

## Pro — ~$12–25/user/month

Pricing depends on AI usage. Includes:

- Unlimited workspaces
- Higher AI limits
- Voice mode
- Advanced diagrams
- Version history
- Exports

## Team — ~$20–40/user/month

Includes everything in Pro, plus:

- Collaboration
- Shared workspaces
- Permissions
- Team libraries
- Admin controls

## Enterprise — custom pricing

Includes:

- SSO
- SCIM
- Audit logs
- Advanced security
- Private deployment / VPC options
- Custom AI policies
