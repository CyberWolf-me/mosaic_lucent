A canvas with meaning, not just shapes.

Every object on a Lucent board knows what it is. That is what lets the AI edit rather than regenerate, what keeps the board editable when the conversation ends, and what lets one diagram become a presentation, a wireframe, or a document without a rebuild.

  • Talk, don't draw. The primary interaction is describing; manual editing is always there when you want it.
  • Every AI action is reversible. An operation log with undo/redo — treat your board's history like Git.
  • One model, many views. Mind map to flowchart to architecture to wireframe: transformations, not re-authoring.
  • Real-time multiplayer. Server-authoritative rooms with live cursors and comments; the log is the source of truth.
  • Bring your own model key. Deploy with our provider budget, or let each user bring their own.

How it works

  1. Describe — type or talk. Speech goes through transcription into the same text pipeline.
  2. Structured operations — the AI emits validated operations (create node, connect, transform), never pixels or coordinates.
  3. Layout — a deterministic engine places the graph. Models are bad at coordinates; we don't ask them.
  4. Refine — every follow-up references the board's real state, so “make it simpler” works.

Your boards are your data.

Board content is never used to train models. Provider keys live only on the server. Every request is tenant-scoped, every operation is validated before it touches your board, and a deployment can run on your own infrastructure with Postgres-backed persistence.
